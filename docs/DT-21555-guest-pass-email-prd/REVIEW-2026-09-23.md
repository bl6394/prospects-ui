# Review: DT-21555 Guest Pass email handoff against the Guest Pass v1 project (2026-09-23)

Scope reviewed: `guest-pass-email-database-handoff.{md,html,docx,pdf}` (same content), `guest-pass-email-jira-drafts.md`, `sql/*.sql`. Compared against PRD v3.6 Rounds 5–7 (D48–D74), Change Requests v3, `docs/sprints/SPRINT-0004.md` (contract v4, register CF-13/14/15, RQ-0, RQ-3) and the BFF's DataTrak service-layer adapter (`adapters/platform/datatrakServiceCatalog.ts`).

## What the handoff covers well

- Catalog-item model matches D61/D70: flag + visits + contract days on `CATALOGITEMS`, copied to `SALEITEMS` on deploy, product items, 1–100 validation. `'0'/'1'` boolean convention and defaults are explicit.
- Liability (waiver) language as a club field next to the prospect terms (D50/D61): `SPL_EXTENSIONS.SPLEX_PROSPECT_LIABILITY`, editable for Fast Add and Online.
- Email: static QR of the guest barcode, terms and liability text in the body, subject/header/footer/active per club through the existing Email Types UI (D63, CR-19, CR-24). Email data script is rerunnable; verification queries and rollback are written down.
- Story 4 fires the email on a **completed** sale, not on prospect creation, and voids/returns do not trigger it.

## Gaps for this project (ordered by impact on Guest Pass v1)

### G1 — The web-service layer is not part of the handoff (blocks CF-13 / CF-14)
The scripts add columns, but nothing exposes them through the DataTrak `.spr` layer the Guest Pass page reads (the layer MICO uses). The BFF adapter reads `saleItems.spr` and `getClubDefaultExternal.spr` and needs, per sale item: `guestPassOnline` (from `SAI_GUEST_PASS`), `contractDays`, `visits`; per club: the liability text (`SPLEX_PROSPECT_LIABILITY`) alongside `prospectTerms`, and the club **timezone** (not in the schema today; the adapter fails closed without it). Until those fields appear on the responses, dev runs on synthetic fixtures and the live catalog read stays blocked. Ask: add the three sale-item fields and the liability field to the two responses in the same DT change, and confirm names.

### G2 — `saleItems.spr` requires a member id (CF-14)
Verified on 2026-09-18: `GET …/pos/saleItems.spr?club=…` → 400 "Required parameter 'memberId' is not present"; with an empty member id → 200 with `saleItems: null`. The Guest Pass page lists offers **before** anyone is identified. Ask: a club-scoped variant (or make `memberId` optional for the guest-pass listing).

### G3 — Who sets the prospect's access window for a register sale?
The BFF sets begin = today, expiration = today + contract days − 1, visits from the item on the create/repaint call (D62/D71). Story 4 sends an email after a POS sale but does not say the sale sets the **purchaser's prospect begin/expiration/visits** from `SAI_GUEST_PASS_CONTRACT_DAYS/VISITS`. Without that, a register-sold pass has an email and QR but no access window (door enforcement is by the prospect record — D48/D62). Ask: define the POS behaviour, or state that register sales are out of scope for v1.

### G4 — Prospect create/update/deactivate contract still missing (CF-15, blocks live purchase)
Nothing here covers the `addProspect` fields for begin/expiration/visits/item/opt-in, idempotency, or a deactivate / conditional update used by create → pay → deactivate (D65). Also: MICO maps one consent value to four `comm*` flags — the BFF sends one `marketingOptIn` boolean.

### G5 — Two emails on the web path, one of them before payment (RQ-0)
The web flow creates the prospect **before** payment. If `ADD_PROSPECT` still fires on creation, a declined buyer receives the prospect email (terms, welcome) before the deactivate lands; the QR only in `ADD_GUEST` after the sale limits the damage but does not remove the pre-payment email. Ask: can the online create suppress `ADD_PROSPECT` (flag on the call), or is the prospect email acceptable for a declined buyer? Also confirm the **online** sale recorded by the BFF (`/pos/onlineSale.spr` or its successor) is a "completed POS sale" for Story 4's trigger and that "the member on the sale" resolves to the prospect with the email the buyer typed.

### G6 — Email content vs PRD D52 / FR-P20
Story 3 lists contact details, terms, liability text and the QR. PRD asks additionally for: the **pass window** (begin and expiration or "use by", visits — CR-19 "pass date"), the **marketing opt-in acknowledgement** text and checked state (D67; TCPA record), and an unmistakable subject (CR-24). `Your Club Pass` is generic and identical to what a member might read as membership mail; the plan's stand-in used "Your Day Pass at {club}". Ask: add the window/visits line and the opt-in acknowledgement; consider a subject that names the pass.

### G7 — This is a new email type, which the PRD had ruled out
D52 and §9 say "existing prospect email type, modified — no new email type". The handoff adds `ADD_GUEST`. It may be the better design (it decouples the QR from creation — see G5), but the PRD, FR-P20 and the BFF's delivery vocabulary ("prospect email", `renderer: 'dt'`) should be updated to say "Add Guest email", and the reason recorded.

### G8 — Liability text field: size and format
`VARCHAR2(4000)` is 4000 **bytes**: a legal waiver plus the club's own paragraphs can exceed it (the TCPA opt-in paragraph alone is ~900 characters), and multi-byte characters count double. Consider `CLOB` or at least `VARCHAR2(4000 CHAR)` with an explicit UI limit. Also define whether the text is plain or HTML: the email renders it and the web page shows it in a dialog (the BFF's adapter decodes the `prospectTerms` base64-HTML convention — the new field should follow the same convention, and club-authored HTML must be sanitised on the web side).

### G9 — Full-table UPDATEs in the DDL script
`project-guest-pass-catalog-item.sql` adds the columns, then `UPDATE catalogItems …` / `UPDATE saleItems …` over every row, then sets the defaults. On large `SALEITEMS` tables that is a long-running, redo-heavy update with row locks during the deployment window. `ALTER TABLE … ADD (col CHAR(1) DEFAULT '0')` is metadata-only on Oracle 11g+ and achieves the same end state without the UPDATE (add `NOT NULL` if desired). The ALTERs are also not rerun-safe (no guard), unlike the email script.

### G10 — Returns, voids and access
Story 4 excludes returned/voided lines from sending the email, but nothing revokes access when a sold pass is returned at the register (the prospect keeps begin/expiration/visits). Web refunds are a PRD non-goal (D15/D22); register returns are not. Ask: does a return deactivate/repaint the prospect?

### G11 — Recipient and eligibility
Story 4's recipient is "the member on the sale". PRD D12 blocks active/frozen/past-due/banned **members** from buying passes and D13 rules out buying for someone else. Fast Add "add guest" is a member bringing a guest. Clarify: for a guest-pass sale the recipient must be the **prospect** (the guest), and a member-type payer should not receive a guest QR.

### G12 — Product-only enforcement
D70: guest pass is a **product**, never a service. The columns sit on `CATALOGITEMS` for all item types; the doc says "product catalog item" but the enforcement (UI/API) is not stated. The BFF also needs an item-type field on the sale-item response to skip services.

### G13 — v1 single-visit constraint
DT allows visits 1–100; the Guest Pass page refuses items with visits ≠ 1 in v1 (D72) and shows contract days as the window. The enablement guide (CR-21) should say "v1 online: visits = 1" so a club does not flag a 5-visit item and wonder why the page hides it.

### G14 — Deployment details
- `CTEM_UNIQUE_BILLING` collision on `030` is called out; the fixed `CTEM_ID` GUID `7E2E04A6…` must also be unused in every environment.
- `contactTemplateLocation` rows are cloned only for locations that already have `ADD_PROSPECT`; clubs without it get no Add Guest row (and therefore no email) — say so in the enablement steps.
- The Jira drafts point to `docs/database/...` paths that differ from this folder, spell the assignee "Chancey" (Chancy elsewhere), and reference a `NOJIRA` branch — tidy before filing.

## Concerns outside this handoff's scope but on the same critical path
- Pay Page: the standalone `guestpass.dev.abcfitness.net` origin is refused by Pay Page (400) and the fixture clubs are unknown to the QA tenant (500) — payments team, not DT.
- LaunchDarkly flag `guest-pass-online`, DT service credential in the BFF secret, Cloudflare on the Guest Pass host — platform/PK.

4. Add pass window/visits and the opt-in acknowledgement to the email; revisit the subject. (G6)
5. `CLOB` or CHAR semantics for the liability field; declare plain vs HTML. (G8)
6. Replace ADD + UPDATE + MODIFY DEFAULT with `ADD … DEFAULT`. (G9)
7. Update PRD D52/FR-P20 and the BFF delivery vocabulary for the new email type. (G7)
